## **What it is**:

The app is basically an STL file viewer, where users can upload an STL file and visualize it in 3D. You can rotate, zoom in and out, and see the object from different angles. Based on the object's volume, the app can calculate how much it would cost to print it on a 3D printer.

## **Why**:

My company (Folhastech) has a 3d printer, we got asked many times to give quotes, as I had this project already from a time where I had a 3d printer, i just adapted it to the company's needs.
